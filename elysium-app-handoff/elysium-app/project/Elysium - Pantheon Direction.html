IDiagnosis · how the product drifted

Two wrong turns, in sequence

The app is not broken. It is mis-aimed. Each correction so far solved the last problem by creating the next one. The pattern, laid out plainly:

OriginThe seed
A vanilla-JS skincare tracker. Genuinely useful, narrow, and honest. The 3-night ingredient cycle still anchors everything — it is the one surface a user would notice missing within a day.
Soul addedRight instinct
Greek myth arrived as the soul of the app — Temple, ritual, patron, chronicle. This was correct. It gave a hygiene app a reason to be quiet, premium, and lived-in instead of another optimization scorecard.
Drift oneDecoration
Then came ritual surfaces that were beautiful but did nothing — tap-to-witness, tap-to-hold, tap-to-release. Lovely copy, no payoff. The myth became ornament. A god you tap and nothing changes is set dressing, not structure.
Drift twoErosion
The fix for decoration was utility — Today intentions, Care notes, reaction memory, flows. Useful. But usefulness arrived by thinning the mythology: Water demoted, Light reduced to a tone-picker, domains becoming labels on ordinary features. The soul started leaving to make room for the tool.

The mistake in both turns is the same: treating mythology and utility as a trade-off — a slider where moving toward one means away from the other.

They are not opposites. The error is structural, not tonal. Decorative myth fails because it has no job. Generic utility fails because its job has no form. The correction is to make every patron own a job, and let the patron shape how that job is done. When that holds, adding utility deepens the myth instead of eroding it.

IICorrected doctrine · the patron test

A patron is the reason a feature has its shape

Not a theme applied afterward. Not a name on a button. A patron is the human need a surface exists to serve, and the discipline that decides how it serves it. To survive, every surface passes two gates.

Gate one

The Utility Gate

Does this surface do a job the user would genuinely miss if it vanished tomorrow? Strip the Greek name entirely. Is what remains still the best tool for that job?

Fails as decoration. If removing the name leaves a gesture with no practical residue — a tap that records nothing the user needs, a ritual whose only output is more copy — the surface is ornament. Cut it.

Gate two

The Form Gate

Does the patron shape how the job is done — its hierarchy, naming, timing, restraint, the moment of the day it belongs to? Could only this patron hold this surface?

Fails as generic utility. If the surface would be identical in any to-do or wellness app — same layout, same voice, same logic — the myth is a sticker. Re-shape it until the patron is load-bearing.

Decoration passes Form but fails Utility. Generic software passes Utility but fails Form. Elysium requires both — that is the entire correction.

This sits above the Continuity Constitution, not against it. The constitution governs what continuity may never become (a counter, a streak, a dashboard). The Patron Test governs whether a surface deserves to exist at all. A surface must pass both: the Patron Test for purpose, the Six Tests for how it remembers.

Practically, the test reorganizes the whole product around a simple claim: each patron owns one real human need and one moment of the day. The myth is not the wallpaper of the room — it is the floor plan.

IIIRevised architecture · the spine & the arc

Four tabs. One day-arc. Eight patrons.

Keep the four-tab spine — it is correct and shipped. Do not add patron tabs; the nav is not the Pantheon. The patrons live in time (the day-arc) and in place (Temple). The tabs answer four different questions.

Tab 01 · first Today Kairos — the present moment "What does now ask of me?" The front door. Stays first.
Tab 02 · anchor Care Hygieia — the body kept "What does my skin need tonight?" The practical anchor. Stays.
Tab 03 · the room Temple the Pantheon entire "Where do all the patrons stand today?" The whole room, arranged by the arc.
Tab 04 · the house Workshop mortal back-of-house Account, cues, data, install. Deliberately un-patroned — the one honest seam.
The day-arc — patrons mapped to time

This is the real structure. Today and Temple both read from it. Each patron surfaces when its hour comes, and recedes when it passes. No patron is ever "behind."

First light Apollo Light → Care Name the day; protect it. SPF threshold before you step out.
Morning Kairos Today Place 1–3 intentions. Receive what yesterday left.
Midday Athena Mind Offload one thread. Turn a worry into an intention, or set it down.
Evening Hygieia · Asclepius Care · Recovery Night protocol. If skin reacted, Asclepius pulls the actives back.
Night Mnemosyne · Hypnos Chronicle · Sleep Write what remains. Decide what carries into tomorrow. Close the day.

On the four pillars question. The brief floats Care / Today / Chronicle / Sleep as the main pillars. Three of those are right; Sleep is not a pillar — it is an endpoint, used once a night, and does not earn a permanent tab. Chronicle (Mnemosyne) is precious enough to deserve prominence, but it earns it inside Temple as the night's anchor patron, not as a fourth tab that sits empty for most of the day. The spine stays: Now, Anchor, Room, House.

On "should domains become patrons." Yes — every domain becomes a patron with a job, but the patron name is never the tappable label. You enter "Care," subtitled Hygieia; you enter "Mind," subtitled Athena. The god is the form and the why; the plain word is the door.

IVThe Pantheon · patron-by-patron design system

Eight patrons, each with a job

For each: the practical job it owns, its visual role, the interactions it is allowed, what must be removed or avoided, and how it appears in the UI. Two patrons are corrections to existing domains; one is a retirement.

Anchor · keep

Hygieia

Care

The practical anchor. The reason the app survives a day of disuse everywhere else.

Practical job
The skincare protocol: morning/night routine, the 3-night active-ingredient cycle (niacinamide · salicylic · rest), skin check-in, reaction notes, irritation memory.
Visual role
The only featured surface — bronze-gradient hero card, the warmest material in the app. Care is allowed elevation no other patron gets, because it carries real consequence (active acids on skin).
Allowed
Step completion, cycle position display, skin condition chips, reaction note, the §19A practical calendar, "last time on this night" reaction memory.
Avoid
Streaks, adherence %, "you kept 4 of 7." Care has a practical history exception — it is not a license for scorecards.
Appears as
The Care tab hero + tonight's protocol; the day-arc evening anchor; the featured card on Temple.
Front door · keep

Kairos

Today

Not chronological time — the opportune moment. The present, asking to be met.

Practical job
What matters today: 1–3 intentions in the user's own words, yesterday's carryover, the open loops the present hour holds.
Visual role
A threshold, not a list. Masthead + naked separator rows on the obsidian floor. The front door identity: mono date, DM Sans heading, Spectral accent noun.
Allowed
Add / keep / release intention; one-day quiet carryover echo; time-aware open rows that point to the hour's patrons (§19B).
Avoid
Reading like a generic todo app. No "tasks," no priority, no due dates, no counts, no progress bar. Kairos holds the present — it does not schedule the future.
Appears as
Tab 01, first surface on open. Detailed redesign in §V.
Memory · keep

Mnemosyne

Chronicle

The deep well. Memory itself — not a journal feature, the domain of remembering.

Practical job
Writing, archive, plain substring search, and resurfacing — the room returning a line you forgot writing. The most precious data in the app.
Visual role
Spectral-italic writing surface; dashed bronze prompt card; faint resurfaced lines that age qualitatively. Quiet, ink-and-paper, never database chrome.
Allowed
Write; read reverse-chronologically; literal search; the Well surfaces one entry by date/cycle math (§15.4A, §11).
Avoid
Tags, folders, word counts, sentiment, "N entries about X," relevance ranking. Mnemosyne remembers — she does not analyze.
Appears as
Temple's night-anchor patron; reachable from Today's open rows at evening/night. The most prominent secondary patron.
Closure · keep

Hypnos

Sleep

Closure. The patron who lets the app — and the day — go quiet.

Practical job
Shutdown: the parking note, deciding what carries into tomorrow, the night-to-morning handoff. The one surface that ends the day.
Visual role
A darkening, narrowing ritual. Closure invitations, one optional parking line, the carry / let-pass decision on unkept intentions.
Allowed
Closure mark; parking note (persists); carry/pass routing of Today's unkept items into tomorrow's carryover (§19C).
Avoid
Becoming a sleep tracker. No bedtime stats, no duration, no consistency graph, no "you slept X." The app does not know — and must never ask.
Appears as
Day-arc endpoint (night); reachable from Today's open rows after dusk; closes into the next morning's Kairos handoff.
Clarity · keep

Athena

Mind

Clarity. Not stillness for its own sake — the work of choosing one thread.

Practical job
Mental-load offload: place a worrying thread in the room to relieve occupation, then either send it to Today as an intention or set it down. Turning thought into a decision.
Visual role
A single calm offload surface — one textarea, three plain actions. Spartan, like a clear desk. Already correct as of EA-178B; protect it.
Allowed
Hold one thread for the day; "Set it down" / "Send to Today" / "Clear." Expires at day-end (§18.8).
Avoid
A productivity timer. No countdown, no session length, no "minutes held," no breath-streak. The 60s floor was structure, not a goal — do not resurrect duration UI.
Appears as
Day-arc midday; the bridge between Mind and Today — the only patron that writes directly into Kairos.
Light · rebuild

Apollo

Light

Daylight and the sun. The weak mapping today; the strong one tomorrow.

Practical job
Daytime protection. SPF awareness tied to Care's sunscreen step, sun-exposure threshold, the morning go-out moment. The patron of the sun should be about the sun on your skin — not "noticing the morning."
Visual role
An airy morning threshold that carries one practical line: is SPF on before you cross it. The tone-of-morning gesture stays, but rides on top of a real check.
Allowed
Morning entry; an SPF-on acknowledgment that links to Care m2; a sun-threshold cue. Witness marks persist as horizon fade (§16).
Avoid · today's weakness
Apollo is currently a tone-picker (Soft / Clear / Steady / Guarded) with no payoff — pure Drift-one decoration. It fails the Utility Gate. Rebuild it around SPF or fold it into Care's morning.
Appears as
Day-arc first-light, feeding directly into Hygieia's morning protocol.
Body · rebuild → rename

Asclepius

Body → Recovery

Healing. The patron you go to when the protocol hurt you.

Practical job
Skin recovery & barrier repair. When skin reacts to actives or sun: pull treatment back, simplify, support the barrier. Care is the plan; Asclepius is what to do when the plan irritates. Tightly bound to Hygieia's reaction state.
Visual role
A calm, clinical relief surface — not a wellness stretch screen. Reads as "ease off," the medical-quiet counterpart to Care's active hero.
Allowed
Recovery guidance keyed to today's skin condition; "rest the actives" routing; a same-session grounding pause inherited from the old Body/Water reset.
Avoid · today's weakness
"Body" today is generic desk-stretch (neck / shoulders / wrists) — indistinguishable from any wellness app. It fails the Form Gate. Recovery gives it a job only Elysium's skincare core could justify.
Appears as
Surfaces in Care's night section when condition = irritated; a quiet Temple patron otherwise. Desk-relief survives as a minor affordance, not the soul.
Water · retire

Lethe

Water — retire as standalone

The strongest temptation toward decoration. Resolve it by removing it.

The problem
Water has no job. A hydration counter violates the constitution (counts forbidden). A Lethe "release" mechanic — tap to forget — is the purest example of the decorative ritual the brief warns against: beautiful, and entirely without payoff. Both proposed rescues fail a gate.
The verdict
Retire the standalone domain. EA-179 → EA-183 already demoted and folded most of it; finish the job. Do not revive it as Lethe.
Fold the crumbs
The one useful fragment — hydration — becomes a line inside Hygieia (it already exists: habit "drink 2.5–3L"). The "notice temperature, breathe, continue" grounding pause folds into Asclepius as a recovery micro-action.
Avoid
Keeping a domain alive because it already exists. A patron with no job is the exact failure this correction is fixing.
Appears as
Nowhere, as itself. Its useful matter lives on inside Hygieia and Asclepius.

Today · Kairos · morning state

What changed, and why

Builds directly on the shipped Threshold (EA-171A). The correction is the patron framing, not a teardown.

Heading
From "Today's intentions" to "What the morning asks" — the accent noun is the moment, not the feature. Kairos speaks of the present; the word shifts with the day-arc period (morning / afternoon / evening / night) using logic that already exists.
Intentions
Unchanged in behaviour and form. Naked separator rows, bronze check, Spectral "Placed" label, bare 02 / 03 capacity glyph. This is the useful core and it stays exactly as shipped.
Open rows → patrons
The single biggest change. "Open" becomes "The hour holds", and each open row now carries its patron: a small glyph medallion, the plain job ("Light · protect the day"), and the italic patron name ("Apollo") beneath. The open set stops reading as leftover todos and starts reading as which gods this hour belongs to.
The arc, felt
The rows are ordered and populated by the day-arc: Apollo + Hygieia at morning, Athena at midday, Mnemosyne + Hypnos at night. The user never sees "the arc" named — they just find the right patrons waiting at the right hour.
Carryover
Same one-day echo. Kairos receives what yesterday left; it never accumulates a backlog.

What stays

  • Every §19B behaviour: add, keep, release, carryover
  • The shipped Threshold masthead + naked rows
  • Bronze check, footer voice, no counts

What's corrected

  • Open rows gain patron identity (glyph + name)
  • Heading noun tracks the moment, not the feature
  • Today stops reading as a generic todo list

IXFinal warning · the two ways to fall back

Do not slide back into either ditch

The road has a ditch on both sides. Every future ticket should be checked against both before it ships. If a surface is drifting, it is almost always drifting toward one of these.

Ditch one · the left

Decorative mythology with no utility

  • Adding a "patron" whose only interaction is tap-to-acknowledge with no practical residue.
  • Writing more poetic copy to make a feature feel meaningful instead of giving it a job.
  • Reviving Water (or any retired domain) because the Pantheon "feels incomplete" without it.
  • Naming a god on a surface that would be identical without the name.
  • Building rituals that are beautiful, on-brand, and that no user would miss if deleted.
Ditch two · the right

Generic utility with no mythology

  • Adding a useful feature whose shape is borrowed from a todo or wellness app, with a Greek label pasted on.
  • Demoting or removing a patron to "make room" for usefulness — the Drift-two error.
  • Letting Kairos become a task manager, Athena a timer, or Hypnos a sleep tracker.
  • Introducing counts, streaks, %, or dashboards because they are "just practical" — the constitution still binds.
  • Treating the myth as theming applied last, rather than the floor plan decided first.

Before any new surface ships, ask the two questions in order: Would the user miss this if it vanished? — and — Could only this patron hold it? A yes to both is the only road. A no to either is a ditch.

The myth is not the wallpaper of the room. It is the floor plan.
